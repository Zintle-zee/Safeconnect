# Safeconnect
Academic project repository for safeconnect containing project management documentation, evidence and prototype development materials
### A Community Safety Information and Incident-Reporting Platform

**Project:** SafeConnect Auckland Park  
**Location:** Auckland Park–Melville, Johannesburg, South Africa  
**Course:** ILS2B01 — Project Management  
**Project type:** Academic group project and visual, clickable prototype

## 1. Project Overview

SafeConnect is a proposed community safety information and incident-reporting platform designed for the Auckland Park–Melville area.

The project explores how residents, students living off campus and local businesses could use a shared digital platform to report incidents, view community safety information and access relevant alerts.

The project responds to the limitations of relying exclusively on informal communication channels, where reports can be fragmented, difficult to verify and challenging to organise.

## 2. Problem Statement

Community safety information may be shared through informal messaging groups and word of mouth. This can make it difficult to maintain a structured record of incidents, identify relevant information and distinguish verified reports from potentially misleading information.

SafeConnect proposes a more organised way to present community safety information, with consideration for moderation, privacy and responsible reporting.

## 3. Proposed Solution

The proposed platform explores the following capabilities:

- Incident reporting and categorisation.
- A community safety map and incident information.
- Alerts for relevant safety information.
- Reporting flags and moderation to help address potentially false or malicious reports.
- An administrative dashboard for reviewing and managing reports.

These features describe the proposed solution. Their presence and functionality must be confirmed against the current prototype.

## 4. Prototype

A visual, clickable prototype was developed using Canva to demonstrate the proposed SafeConnect user experience.

The prototype is intended to communicate the concept and illustrate possible user interactions. It should not be interpreted as a fully deployed application or as evidence that live incident reporting, mapping, notifications, authentication or backend integrations are operational.

## 5. Project Management

The group used a hybrid project management approach, combining structured planning and milestone tracking with flexibility to review and refine the proposed solution.

Jira was used to organise and track project activities. Project documentation covers relevant planning, resources and budget, design, quality, risk, feedback, closure and contribution records.

## 6. Quality and Responsible Use

The proposed solution should consider:

- Clear navigation and understandable reporting steps.
- Readable text and accessible interface design.
- Appropriate handling of personal and location information.
- Moderation and review of potentially misleading reports.
- Clear distinctions between sample information and verified incident information.

Any sample incident information in the prototype is for demonstration purposes and must not be interpreted as a verified real-world incident.

## 7. Repository Contents

This repository is intended to organise the project's documentation, requirements, prototype information, project-management artefacts and supporting evidence.

Only files that have been created, reviewed and added to the repository should be represented as available contents.

## 8. Current Limitations

SafeConnect remains an academic project and prototype. Production deployment, live data sources, operational backend services, security controls and real-world integration would require further design, implementation, testing and validation.

## 9. Future Improvements

Potential future work includes:

- Further usability and accessibility testing.
- Refinement based on feedback.
- Stronger privacy and moderation controls.
- Evaluation with intended community users.
- Technical implementation and integration, subject to project scope and resources.
